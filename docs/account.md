# Your account

You can play against the computer without an account. A free **Via Mochi account** adds:

- **Your Collection and decks**, kept in your account, so they're on every device you sign in on.
- **The deck builder**, to make your own decks.
- **Friends**: add them and [play them online](friends.md).
- **Nothing lost** if you lose or change your device: sign in and everything is there.

On the Home screen, **Friend**, **Collection** and **Deck builder** say *Sign in to open* until you have an account.

## Sign in on another device

On the Home screen, tap **Friend**, **Collection** or **Deck builder**. Type your email, tap **Continue**, then type
the code from the email. You're signed in, and your Collection, decks and friends
are all there.

## Manage your account

Tap the **gear** on the Home screen, then **Account** in the list of Settings sections. There you can:

- **Change your Pawtrait**, the picture your friends see.
- **Sign out** on this device. Your account and Collection stay safe.
- **Export my data**, to get a copy of everything your account holds.
- **Delete account**. Your account, Collection, decks and Pawtraits are erased after 30 days. Sign in again before
  then and nothing is lost.

How we look after your data is in the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
