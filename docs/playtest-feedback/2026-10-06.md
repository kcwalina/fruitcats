of 10-03, 10-04 and 10-05 were not reviewed. Their bot gauntlets gave exactly the same numbers as 10-06: the cards
did not change, and the gauntlet uses fixed seeds. Their LLM reports are read here too.

## How the claims were checked

All 10 of last night's games were replayed exactly with the engine, matching the LLM's choices one by one. Every
replay ended with the same winner in the same round. The custom decks were rebuilt from the nightly's seed, and they
match the deck codes in the report. The transcripts hold only the LLM's choices, so the Clash logs come from these
replays.

## Real bugs, fixed

### "Once per round" abilities shared one flag

A unit kept a single "used this round" flag for all its "once per round" abilities, so only the first one ever
happened. In game 9, round 1, Pearl Oyster survived a hit with the Pearl Tears trait on. The log showed one Offering;
the cards give two. Rule 800.4 counts each ability on its own, and the engine now does too.

### The Pearl Tears trait counted per unit

The trait says: "Once per round, when one of your Jiaoren survives damage, gain an Offering." The rulebook (850.6)
says the same. The engine gave each Jiaoren its own copy of the trait's ability, each with its own once-per-round, so
three hurt Jiaoren gave three Offerings (game 9, round 3). The single flag above usually hid this.

Fixing the first bug alone made Jiaoren win 70% in the deploy's balance check. A trait's "once per round" now counts
once for its player. A unit's own abilities, and keywords such as the Pearl Tears an Awakened Zhu'er gives, still
count per unit.

### The bot's stand-in card in generated decks

"random Dziadziuś + Core #4" beat the starters 70% with 4× "Unknown card". That card is the engine's own stand-in: the
bot puts it in place of cards it can't see when it searches ahead. The random-deck maker took its family, "Core", for
a family to build with. No player deck had it (the Store never sells it), but deck checks now refuse it anyway.

### The nightly had no time left for LLM games

The 0.7 games are longer (8.3 rounds, 79 actions). At scale 3 the gauntlet took from 07:00 to 12:43, so the LLM games
got 13 minutes:

### Why the pairings are lopsided

- **Hui Hai against Jiaoren (31%).** Hui Hai wins rounds 4–6. From round 7, Jiaoren wins with more Offerings and
  Levels. The Oldest Weaver decides it: its −2 Power to every enemy, every second bout, cripples Hui Hai's big
  hitters. In this pairing it is worth 30 points to Jiaoren. Hui Hai's only answer is Pebble-Thrower, the 1-cost
  Assassin that reaches Jiaoren's back row.
- **Pari against Hui Hai (33%).** Pari wins rounds 2 and 3 and then loses. From round 4, Stone for Stone at 4 Hui Hai
  and Pebble's Awakening make Hui Hai's units 8/7 or 9/5, and Pebble-Throwers kill Pari's fragile Marksmen behind
  its one Taunt unit. No single Hui Hai card stands out.
- **Jiaoren against Pari (40% after the fixes).** Pari's cheap rush wins the early Clashes; Jiaoren recovers too late.
  Murghatipi (a 1-cost 3/2 Assassin) leads it.

it may play Supports worse than a person would.

## For the owner

1. **Two pairings are still under 38%: Pari against Hui Hai (35%) and Hui Hai against Jiaoren (37%).** The deploy
   gate's floor is 30%, so they ship. Nothing measured fixes Pari against Hui Hai alone. The options:
   - **Leave it.** The starters are 48–53% overall, and a circle of counters isn't bad in itself.
   - **Give Pari a second Taunt unit in the starter** (Schaibar for a Mountain Gale). Measured: Pari against Hui Hai
     43%, but Pari 67% overall. It would need Pari weakened elsewhere: a bigger change to its deck.
2. **Tool-Hiding Alux carries the Aluxes deck (+35 points).** Without it Aluxes wins 2% against Pari. One point of
   Power less drops Aluxes to 34%. A fix means rebuilding the Aluxes starter around more good cards. Recommendation:
   do it with the Aluxito question from the last review (his Awakened ability on any unit). Nothing changed tonight.
3. **Supports and Taunt Tanks lose games for the bot.** Stove Keeper costs Domowiki 17 points, Keeper of the Door 10,
   Night Patrol Alux 6. Either they are weak, or the bot places them badly. Worth a look before balancing them:
   a person may play them better.
4. **Pearl Tears is now once per player, as written.** If it was meant to be once per Jiaoren (much stronger), the
   text should say so. Jiaoren would then need a large nerf: once per Jiaoren gave it 70%.
