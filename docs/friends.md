# Playing with friends

Play a friend on your own phones, tablets or computers. Two steps: add each other, then play.

You each need a free Via Mochi account to play friends: see [Create an account](account.md#create-an-account).

## Add a friend

Two ways: **together**, one phone shows a QR code and the other scans it; or **apart**, you send your code by text and
your friend types it in.

A code works **once**, for **15 minutes**: send it when your friend is ready. Out of time? Show your code again for a new one.
On a computer, **Copy the code** copies the message, ready to paste.

### How to play: the choices

| Choice | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Teaching game** | For a friend who's new: no timer, **What should I do?** hints, **Take back** a move, and either of you can **Show my hand**. It doesn't count in your score. |
| **Pace** | **Relaxed** (2 minutes a move), **Quick** (45 seconds) or **No timer**. Out of time? Nothing happens by itself: your friend can give you more time. |
| **Your Lives** | Start with fewer than 9 Lives, to give your friend a head start. |

### Good to know

- **Left the game?** It waits for you: tap **Friend** on the Home screen to go back to it.
- **"You're number 3 in line"?** Lots of people are playing online right now. Keep the screen open: you'll be let in
  as soon as there's room.
