This policy covers decks and cards bought in the Fruitcats Store on the web (fruitcats.viamochi.com). It explains
how to get your money back, and what happens to the cards when you do.

## Who sells, and who refunds

Purchases on the web are sold by **Paddle.com**, our authorized reseller and the seller of record (see section 5
of the [Terms of Use](terms-of-use.md)). Paddle takes the payment, sends the receipt and makes the refund, under
this policy and [Paddle's buyer terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/checkout-buyer-terms). Purchases made in the
iPhone, Android or Steam apps are refunded by Apple, Google or Valve, under their own policies.

## Refunds within 14 days

- **You can ask for a full refund of any purchase within [14] days of buying it, for any reason.**
- **After [14] days,** ask us anyway if something went wrong: for example, cards that never reached your account.
  We look at each case and refund when it's fair.
- **Bought the same thing twice by mistake?** The second purchase is refunded in full, whenever you notice.
- **Went on sale soon after you bought it?** Within the [14] days, you can ask for a refund and buy it again at the
  sale price.

## How to ask

- **In the game:** Settings, then **Contact us**. Say which purchase (the order number on your Paddle receipt, or
  the date and what you bought).
- **Or through Paddle:** follow the link in your receipt email, or look up the purchase at
  [paddle.net](https://paddle.net).

Refunds go back to the way you paid. Paddle sends them within a few days; your bank may take longer to show them.

## What happens to the cards

A refund ends your license to the cards it covered (section 5 of the [Terms of Use](terms-of-use.md)): they're
removed from your Via Mochi account on every device. A deck that used them stays saved, but can't be played until you
have the cards again. A refund of one item in an order removes only that item's cards.

## EU and UK buyers

The law gives you 14 days to withdraw from a purchase of digital content, unless you agree to receive it at once
and accept that this ends the right. Paddle's checkout asks for that agreement, because the cards arrive in your
account straight away. **We still offer refunds within [14] days, as above.**

## Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a payment with your bank: a refund is quicker for you. A chargeback removes the
cards it covers, like a refund. Using chargebacks or refunds to get cards for free, again and again, can lead to the
account being closed (section 8 of the [Terms of Use](terms-of-use.md)).

## Contact

**Contact us** in the game's Settings. We answer at your account's email address.
