# Card anatomy

What everything on a Fruitcats card means. The card below is **Sanguine, Blood Orange Panther**, a Rare
Cat, in a Prismatic print.

![Sanguine, Blood Orange Panther, with its parts numbered](guide/card-anatomy.webp)

## The parts of a card

| # | Part | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cost** | How many Treats you spend to play the card. A Hero Cat has a star here instead: it's always in play, so it's never paid for. |
| 2 | **Name** | The card's name. Cats, and a Hero Cat's two faces, also have a title under it (*Blood Orange Panther*). |
| 3 | **Art** | The illustration. It never carries rules or other information: the art belongs to the artist. |
| 4 | **Type line** | What kind of card it is (Hero Cat, Cat, Critter, Trick or Toy) and its family (Citrus, Orchard, Tropical or Garden). A Hero Cat's type line also says which face you're looking at: Kitten or Big Cat. |
| 5 | **Rarity mark** | How rare the card is: see [Rarity](#rarity). |
| 6 | **Card number** | The card's number in its set: SB1 is the Starter Box. |
| 7 | **Finish code** | How this copy is printed: see [Finishes](#finishes). Only special prints have one. |
| 8 | **Rules text** | What the card does. **Bold** words are keywords, explained in the [rulebook](rulebook.md#10-keywords). |
| 9 | **Flavour text** | A line in *italics* that tells you something about the card. It has no effect on the game. |
| 10 | **Power** | The paw: how much damage the card deals when it fights. |
| 11 | **Health** | The heart: how much damage defeats it. Tricks and Toys have neither; a Hero Cat has Power only as a Big Cat. |
| 12 | **Frame** | The card's border, in its family's colours, or in the material of a special print. |

Items 5, 6 and 7 sit together at the right end of the type line: the **collector line**. That's where
you look to see how special a card is.

Some cards have a green four-leaf clover in the corner of their art: they're **Lucky** (see the
[rulebook](rulebook.md#9-nine-lives-lucky-and-growing-up)).

## Rarity

Every card has a rarity, printed as a mark in its collector line. All copies of a card share it.
Rarity never changes how a card plays: it's there to collect.

| Mark | Rarity | In the Starter Box |
|---|---|---|
| bronze circle | **Common** | Critters that cost 3 or less |
| silver diamond | **Uncommon** | Critters that cost 4 or more, every Trick and Toy |
| gold star | **Rare** | the named Cats |
| purple crown | **Legendary** | the Hero Cats |

## Finishes

A finish is how one particular copy is printed, like the shiny cards in other card games. The same
card can exist in every finish, and a finish never changes how the card plays. A special print has its
whole frame in its material (the outer border, the border around the art, the edges of the name banner
and type line, and the ring around the cost) and its **finish code** at the end of the collector line.

| Code | Finish | How it looks |
|---|---|---|
| (none) | **Standard** | The frame in the family's colours. |
| **F** | **Foil** | A frame of holographic silver with rainbow flashes. In the app, a rainbow sheen follows your finger. |
| **G** | **Gold** | A frame of polished gold. In the app, a warm light sweeps across the card. |
| **P** | **Prismatic** | The rarest: a rainbow frame all the way round. In the app, a stronger sheen, twinkles, and a glow that runs through every colour. |

The code is also printed on [wallpapers](wallpapers.md), so you can always tell a special print, even
in a picture. In the [Collection](collection.md#finishes), your copies appear in their finish.
