# Wallpapers

Any card in your [Collection](collection.md) can become a wallpaper for your phone, tablet or computer.
The whole screen becomes the card, with its border curving along your screen's rounded corners.
Foil, Gold and Prismatic cards keep their [finish](collection.md#finishes).

No website or web game is allowed to change your wallpaper by itself, so it takes two steps: Fruitcats
makes the picture, then you set it as your wallpaper.

## Make a wallpaper

1. Open the **Collection**. Press and hold a card in your Showcase (right-click on a computer), or
   tap any card in **All cards**.
2. Tap **Make wallpaper**.
3. Choose **Phone**, **Tablet** or **Computer**. Fruitcats picks the one you're using, sized exactly
   for your screen.
4. Tap **Save picture**.

What happens next depends on your device.

## iPhone and iPad

After **Save picture**, your iPhone shows the share sheet. There are two ways from here: the simple way
works on every iPhone, and the Shortcut takes a minute to set up once, then needs only one tap.

### The simple way

1. In the share sheet, tap **Save Image**. The picture goes into your Photos.
2. Go to your Lock Screen, then **touch and hold** it until your wallpapers appear.
3. Tap the **+** button.
4. Tap **Photos** and choose the picture you just saved (it's the newest one).
5. Tap **Add**, then tap **Set as Wallpaper Pair**.

The picture is already the right size, so don't zoom or crop it: that keeps the card's border lined up
with the corners of your screen.

### With a Shortcut

Set up a shortcut once, and after that every wallpaper takes a single tap in the share sheet. You make
the shortcut in Apple's **Shortcuts** app, which comes with every iPhone and iPad.

**Set it up (once)**

1. Open the **Shortcuts** app and tap **+** at the top right. A new, empty shortcut opens.
2. Tap **Add Action**, type **wallpaper** in the search field, and tap **Set Wallpaper Photo**.
3. In the action you just added, tap the blue word for the picture (**Image** or **Photo**) and choose
   **Shortcut Input**.
4. Tap the small arrow at the right of the action to see its options. Turn off **Show Preview**, so it
   doesn't ask you to confirm each time, and turn off **Crop to Subject**.
5. Tap the **ⓘ** button to open **Details**, turn on **Show in Share Sheet**, then tap **Done**.
6. Now name it: tap **New Shortcut** at the top, tap **Rename**, type **Fruitcats Wallpaper**, and tap
   **Done**.
7. Tap **Done** to save the shortcut.

**Every time after that**

1. In Fruitcats, tap **Make wallpaper**, then **Save picture**.
2. In the share sheet, scroll down and tap **Fruitcats Wallpaper**. That's it.

The first time, your iPhone asks whether the shortcut may change your wallpaper: tap **Allow**.

> The Shortcuts app's buttons move a little between iOS versions; these steps are written for iOS 26.
> On some iOS 18 versions, Apple's wallpaper action can fail now and then. If the shortcut shows an
> error, use [the simple way](#the-simple-way) instead.

## Android

1. After **Save picture**, choose where to save the picture (**Photos**, **Gallery** or **Files**).
2. Open the picture in your Photos or Gallery app, tap **⋮** (More), then **Set as wallpaper** (on some
   phones: **Use as** → **Wallpaper**).
3. Choose the Lock screen, the Home screen, or both.

The exact names differ a little between phone makers.

## Computer

**Save picture** puts the picture in your **Downloads** folder. (In Safari on a Mac a share menu may
open instead: save the picture from there.)

- **Mac:** in Finder, Control-click (right-click) the picture and choose **Set Desktop Picture**.
- **Windows:** in File Explorer, right-click the picture and choose **Set as desktop background**.
