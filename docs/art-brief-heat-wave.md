## The set in a few lines

Heat Wave is about **chili peppers**, and it's aimed at **teenage boys**.
Its deck, **Five Alarm**, is full of brawlers that get stronger when they're hurt. The hotter the pepper, the
tougher the card: a mild Pepperoncini is a small bat, and the Carolina Reaper is the most expensive card in
the set. Three cards are also sold on their own: Jack (a Halloween knight), Nova (a space explorer) and Reaper.

**Tone: cool, not cute.** Confident smirks, narrowed eyes, dynamic action poses, low heroic camera angles. A
darker palette (charcoal, crimson, ember orange) with fire, sparks, glowing embers and smoke as accents.
Streetwear, ninja gear and armour all fit.

## Card by card

### The Hero Cat: Blaze, the Chili Bombay

A Bombay is a black cat that looks like a small panther. Blaze is the Hero Cat of the Five Alarm deck, so its
two pictures are the deck's **main pictures**: please draw them first, and sign both in a corner.

#### `HW1-H01-kitten.webp`: Blaze, Ember Kit

- **Tier:** Deck card · **Style:** Sticker
- **Our idea for this picture:** a small plush kitten, glossy jet-black with bright amber eyes, sitting with a mischievous
  smirk and holding up one stubby paw with a tiny flame flickering on it like a lighter. A few embers float
  around.
- **Things we had in mind:** jet-black cat; a **long, curved chili-pepper hood**, glossy red, running from a green stem cap
  on its head down its back to a thin curled tip (clearly a chili, not round like a tomato).
- **Also appears on:** Showdown (HW1-P10), Ring of Fire (HW1-P11)
- **Pawtrait:** yes, see below.

#### `HW1-H01-bigcat.webp`: Blaze, Wildfire

- **Tier:** Deck card · **Style:** Sticker
- **Our idea for this picture:** the same character, bigger and bolder, with glowing amber eyes: a power pose, chest out,
  fists on hips, a ring of cartoon flames swirling around its feet, a cocky grin.
- **Things we had in mind:** as the Kitten. The two faces read as one character.

### Pepper Critters

Each animal takes its colours from its pepper. The hotter the pepper, the bigger and tougher the animal.

#### `HW1-P01.webp`: Pepperoncini Bat

- **Our idea for this picture:** a small bat with pale yellow-green wings shaped like curvy pepperoncini peppers, hanging
  upside down from a charred branch, grinning with two tiny cartoon fangs and glowing orange eyes, embers
  drifting past.
- **Things we had in mind:** a small bat; pepperoncini yellow-green.

#### `HW1-P02.webp`: Chili Chihuahua

- **Our idea for this picture:** a tiny chihuahua with bright chili-red fur, a curly green stem-shaped tuft on its head and
  enormous ears, chest puffed out, barking fearlessly at something much bigger off-screen, a small puff of
  cartoon flame from its mouth, standing on a rock.
- **Things we had in mind:** tiny and fearless; chili red.

#### `HW1-P03.webp`: Jalapeño Honey Badger

- **Our idea for this picture:** a stocky honey badger with glossy jalapeño-green fur and a creamy white stripe down its back,
  a green stem tuft on its head, walking straight at the viewer through a low wall of flames, face clearly
  visible, cocky toothy grin and half-lidded unbothered eyes, sparks bouncing off it.
- **Things we had in mind:** honey badger; jalapeño green; unbothered by the fire.

#### `HW1-P04.webp`: Serrano Wolf

- **Our idea for this picture:** a lean wolf with dark green serrano-coloured fur and red pepper-shaped flame markings on its
  back and legs, on a rocky ledge facing the viewer with a fierce toothy snarl-grin, glowing ember-orange eyes,
  strong orange rim light, a huge red full moon behind it, sparks swirling up.
- **Things we had in mind:** wolf; serrano dark green with red markings. Its flavour text is about howling at the moon.

#### `HW1-P05.webp`: Chipotle Raccoon

- **Our idea for this picture:** a burly raccoon with smoky dark red-brown fur and a black bandit mask, standing guard with
  arms crossed in front of a glowing campfire, smoke curling around it, tough and unimpressed.
- **Things we had in mind:** raccoon; smoky chipotle red-brown; a guarding pose (the card's keyword is Guardian).

#### `HW1-P06.webp`: Habanero Bull

- **Our idea for this picture:** a muscular young bull with a glossy orange hide, head lowered, ready to charge, stamping one
  hoof, steam and little flames snorting from its nostrils, dust flying.
- **Things we had in mind:** bull; habanero orange.

#### `HW1-P07.webp`: Scotch Bonnet Rhino

- **Our idea for this picture:** an armoured rhino with a crinkled red-orange hide textured like a Scotch bonnet pepper,
  charging straight at the viewer at full speed, a trail of dust and flames behind it, fierce determined eyes.
- **Things we had in mind:** rhino; Scotch bonnet red-orange; charging.

#### `HW1-P08.webp`: Dragon's Breath Komodo

- **Our idea for this picture:** a large Komodo dragon with deep crimson scales and glowing ember-orange spots, rearing up on
  its back legs and breathing a big plume of cartoon fire into the sky.
- **Things we had in mind:** a Komodo dragon (a lizard), **no wings**; breathing fire.

### Pepper Cats

Cats have names and personalities. These are still deck cards, but give them a little more character.

#### `HW1-P13.webp`: Jolokia, Ghost Pepper Ninja

- **Our idea for this picture:** a sleek, pale, ghostly-white cat dressed as a ninja, with a red ghost-pepper-shaped hood mask
  and a long flowing red scarf, leaping through the air in a spinning kick, a faint see-through afterimage
  trailing behind it.
- **Things we had in mind:** ninja; ghost-white; the ghost-pepper hood.

#### `HW1-P14.webp`: Ancho, Brawl Boss

- **Our idea for this picture:** a big, muscular dark-brown cat in a deep red ancho-chili bandana and boxing hand wraps,
  standing in a fighting ring with fists raised and a confident grin, sparks flying, crowd lights behind.
- **Things we had in mind:** the boss of the brawlers; a boxer.

#### `HW1-P15.webp`: Naga, the Molten Tiger

- **Our idea for this picture:** a huge tiger whose orange fur glows like molten lava, with black stripes and glowing magma
  cracks running through its coat, roaring on top of a volcanic rock while lava erupts behind it.
- **Things we had in mind:** tiger; molten-lava coat. (Naga is "she" in the rules notes.)

### The single cards: a chance to show off

These three cards are sold on their own, so people buy them to keep and show off. See "Card tiers" in the artist
guide for what has worked well.

#### `HW1-X01.webp`: Jack, the Pumpkin Knight (Foil, $1.49)

- **Tier:** Foil single · **Style:** Painted, a chance to show off · **Family:** Garden, in the Halloween pumpkin-patch world
- **Our idea for this picture:** a fearless cat knight with sleek black fur, glowing pumpkin-orange eyes and a confident
  smirk, face fully visible. Ornate dark iron armour trimmed with glowing pumpkin-vine patterns, a shoulder guard
  carved from a pumpkin, a long tattered midnight-blue cape in the wind. He stands on a pile of glowing
  jack-o'-lanterns, holding a blazing jack-o'-lantern high like a shield or lantern. Low heroic angle, strong
  orange backlight, bats across a giant harvest moon.
- **Things we had in mind:** a knight with a **glowing jack-o'-lantern** (it becomes the token when he's defeated).
- **Also appears on:** the Jack-o'-Lantern token (HW1-K01) could be the lantern he carries.

#### `HW1-X02.webp`: Nova, the Starfruit Voyager (Gold, $4.99)

- **Tier:** Gold single · **Style:** Painted, a chance to show off · **Family:** Tropical, in the deep-space world
- **Our idea for this picture:** a daring cat astronaut, face fully visible through an open golden visor, sleek silver-grey
  fur, bright determined eyes and a confident grin. A glossy white-and-gold spacesuit with a glowing
  **five-pointed starfruit emblem** on the chest. Rocketing forward through space in a dynamic flying pose, one
  paw outstretched holding a blazing golden star, a comet trail of golden light behind. Background: a huge
  swirling magenta, teal and gold nebula and a ringed tropical-green planet.
- **Things we had in mind:** starfruit (the star shape) as the fruit; the face visible, since it's a card to show off.
- **Pawtrait:** yes, see below.

#### `HW1-X03-kitten.webp` and `HW1-X03-bigcat.webp`: Reaper, the Carolina Sphynx (Signature, $19.99)

The most expensive card in the set, sold only in the Signature print (a black stone frame with glowing lava
cracks). A Sphynx is the hairless cat with big ears and a stare. This is a Hero Cat, so it's in the **sticker
style**, pushed to the set's most striking art.

- **Tier:** Signature · **Style:** Sticker, a chance to show off · **Backdrop:** plain deep black-red, a big glowing fiery
  halo behind the cat, a few drifting golden embers
- **Kitten (Smolder Kit):** a small plush kitten, sitting with a sly half-lidded smirk and glowing ember-orange
  eyes, a small flame dancing on the tip of its tail.
- **Big Cat (Final Heat):** bigger and imposing, standing tall with stubby arms crossed and a cool, confident
  smirk, a flowing crimson cape with ember-lit edges, surrounded by a swirling ring of flames and heat shimmer.
- **Things we had in mind:** a **hairless Sphynx** body, smoky charcoal-grey with faint glowing ember cracks like cooling
  lava; big pointed ears; a deep glossy crimson **Carolina Reaper pepper hood**, wrinkled, with a curled pointed
  tail at the back like a scorpion's. Cool and commanding.
- **Pawtrait:** yes, see below.
- **Signature:** your signature is especially welcome on both pictures of this card.

## Pawtraits

All three are drawn in the Legend Pawtrait look described in the artist guide: simple, clean, readable at icon size.
The game adds the ring around them.

### `legend-blaze.webp`: Blaze

- **Comes with:** Blaze, the Chili Bombay (HW1-H01), the Five Alarm deck's Hero Cat
- **Like the card:** jet-black cat, amber eyes, the long glossy red chili-pepper hood with its green stem cap.
- **Expression:** a mischievous, cocky grin. Cool but friendly.
- **Background:** the radiant golden sunburst with a few big white-gold sparkles.

### `legend-nova.webp`: Nova

- **Comes with:** Nova, the Starfruit Voyager (HW1-X02)
- **Like the card:** silver-grey cat, white spacesuit, the star emblem on the chest, the helmet open so the
  face shows.
- **Expression:** wide-eyed and delighted, a happy open smile.
- **Background:** the radiant golden sunburst with a few big white-gold sparkles.

### `legend-reaper.webp`: Reaper

- **Comes with:** Reaper, the Carolina Sphynx (HW1-X03), the Signature card
- **Like the card:** the charcoal Sphynx with ember cracks, big ears, the crimson Carolina Reaper hood with
  its curled tip, ember-orange eyes.
- **Expression:** a cheeky, confident smirk. Keep the skin smooth and simple: no wrinkles.
- **Background:** Reaper's own molten backdrop: ember orange glowing out of black. In the game it gets a ring of
  molten lava.
