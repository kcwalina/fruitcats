## The set in a few lines

Berry Picnic is the holiday release. Its deck, Picnic Club, is a picnic with friends: someone made the plans, everyone brought the wrong snacks, the goose was not invited, and the ants always show up. When a friend heads home, they pass their plate on: that is the deck's mechanic, Leftovers. Three cards sold on their own are wintry and festive.

**Who it's for:** Young adults, mostly young women.

**Style:** Basil's own style. Found family, low stakes, a little feral: cute, silly and a little chaotic, with running jokes (the ants, the goose, the forks nobody brought). The cards sold on their own are a chance to show off.
