Meet Heat

Heat: whenever this unit is dealt damage and survives, it gets +1 Power, up to +3.

Most cats try not to get hurt. Pepper cats go looking for it. Every hit they shrug off makes them stronger, and they'll even singe their own side to turn up the heat.

Wait, peppers are fruit?

They sure are. To a botanist, anything that grows from a flower and carries seeds is a fruit, so a chili belongs in Fruitcats as much as any lemon. (So does the tomato. Just don't tell the salad.)

The leader of the pack

Blaze, the Chili Bombay

Ember Kit. Young Blaze singes one of your own cats to fire it up. On a Heat cat, that spark stays for good.

Growing up. Once five Cats and Critters have fallen in battle, on either side, Blaze becomes Wildfire.

Wildfire. 3 Power, and a choice every round: scorch an enemy, or ignite one of your own for +2 Power.
