# Brief for a lawyer: review of the Fruitcats Terms of Use and Privacy Policy

**What I'm asking for:** a one-time review of two draft documents, before real sign-ups open and before the first
sale. Please mark what must change, suggest wording, and answer the questions below. A fixed-fee quote would help.

**The documents** (drafts, not yet in effect; each ends with the same questions as notes):
- Terms of Use: https://fruitcats.viamochi.com/terms.html
- Privacy Policy: https://fruitcats.viamochi.com/privacy.html

## The product

- **Fruitcats** is a free card game played in a web browser (fruitcats.viamochi.com), and later as free apps on the
  Apple App Store, Google Play and Steam.
- A free **Via Mochi account** (email address, signed in with a one-time code; no password) keeps a player's cards and
  decks on every device, and will enable friends, online play and purchases.
- **What will be sold:** fixed decks and single cards the player sees before buying. No random paid items, no loot
  boxes, no virtual currency.
- **Who runs it:** one person in the United States, with no company, trading as "Via Mochi".
  They would like their personal name kept out of the documents, which name only "Via Mochi (viamochi.com)".

## Who sells

- **I never sell anything; I only license the game and its content.** Every purchase is sold by a third party that is
  the merchant of record: Apple, Google or Valve in their stores, and Paddle on the web (Lemon Squeezy if Paddle
  doesn't accept an individual). They take payment, handle sales tax and VAT, issue receipts and decide refunds.
- When a seller reports a purchase, the server grants the items to the player's account. A refund or chargeback
  removes them.

## Players

- **Worldwide**, including the EU and UK. Data is stored in the US (Microsoft Azure).
- **Ages:** anyone can play solo without an account. Accounts are for 13 and older (birth year asked at sign-up).
  Buying needs 18+ or a parent's approval.
- **Data collected:** email, display name, birth year, game data, security and technical logs. No ads, no trackers,
  no payment details, nothing sold or shared.
- **Contact:** there is no support email address or mailing address. Everyone reaches us through a "Contact us" form
  in the game, open without an account; we answer by email.
- **No marketing emails at launch.** I'd like to send them later, with opt-in.

## Questions

**Seller, identity and contact**
1. Does section 5 correctly describe my role as licensor and the stores and Paddle as sellers, given Paddle's reseller
   terms?
2. Is naming only "Via Mochi (viamochi.com)", with no personal name, enough to identify the contracting party and the
   data controller (GDPR Art. 13)? Should I register "Via Mochi" as a DBA?
3. Is a Contact us form (no email address, no mailing address) enough as the only way to reach us? This covers the
   arbitration opt-out, privacy requests, parents, copyright notices, and EU/UK and app-store contact rules. Must the
   form stay open to people without an account?
4. The App Store's EU trader rules ask for a public address and phone number. What are my options, other than leaving
   out EU storefronts?

**Terms of Use**

5. Arbitration, class-action waiver, 30-day opt-out and small-claims carve-out: are they enforceable? Should it be AAA
   or JAMS?
6. Governing law Washington State, courts in King County: is that right? Is a liability cap of the greater of 12 months
   of purchases or US $50 reasonable?
7. Cards can change after purchase (balance, art, rules), and the game may be shut down with 90 days' notice. Does
   this work for EU/UK players under the Digital Content Directive (Art. 19)? Should I promise an offline mode?
8. EU/UK 14-day withdrawal right: is the wording right, given Paddle's checkout consent?
9. Copyright notices go to Contact us. Do I need to register a DMCA agent with the US Copyright Office? Players can only
   enter display names and deck names today. If I do, can the agent be a title, and does the listing need an email
   address?

**Privacy and minors**

10. Minors: do the 13+ account age gate (by birth year) and parental approval for purchases meet COPPA, US state laws
    and the app stores' rules?
11. EU/UK data transfer to the US: are Microsoft's safeguards (DPA, Standard Contractual Clauses) enough? Do I need an
    EU or UK representative?
12. Is keeping anonymised purchase records indefinitely acceptable under GDPR storage limitation?
13. For future marketing emails: US law (CAN-SPAM) wants a postal address in each one. Is a mailbox-service address or
    PO box enough?

## What I'd like back

- The documents with your changes marked, or a list of required changes with suggested wording.
- Short answers to the questions above.
- Anything missing that a game like this needs before launch.
